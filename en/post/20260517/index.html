A rare, leisurely weekend — slowly doing homework, slowly taking a walk, slowly eating a meal, not rushed at all. Not much homework, no more rage bait pushed to me, comfortably mild weather. It’s been a long time since I’ve had a weekend like this (probably because I stopped scrolling short videos).
Ever since I turned off Bilibili’s personalized recommendations, all I get are high-quality videos, and the endless-scroll feature is basically gone too. Much better than before, when I’d come across things like

How do you read out your phone number? 334 or 533?

—the kind of rage bait that gets a bunch of people arguing furiously underneath.

Short videos… at the table next to us while eating, a kid from grade 2 or 3 saying “That guy walking past is gay”…
There should be limits to meme culture. Do you build your worldview first, or start scrolling short videos first? Perhaps, as Wang Xizhi put it, “Future generations will look upon us just as we look upon the past” — only with time will we know what all this messy stuff really amounts to.
The current obsession with chasing traffic is really not good — people lose sight of some things just for the views.

A leisurely weekend can also be slightly off — a lack of passion for life. But it’s enough.

Keep looking forward, boldly. We have an inexhaustible supply of the future.
