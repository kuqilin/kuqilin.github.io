3.3 Algebra / Number Theory Formulas

$$ \forall x \in \mathbb{R}, \exists y \in \mathbb{N}, \text{ s.t. } y > x $$$$ a_n = a_1 + (n-1)d \quad (\text{general term of arithmetic sequence}) $$$$ S_n = \frac{a_1(1-q^n)}{1-q} \quad (q \neq 1,\ \text{sum of geometric series}) $$$$ \gcd(a,b) \times \operatorname{lcm}(a,b) = |a \times b| $$$$ n! = \prod_{k=1}^{n} k \quad (\text{factorial}) $$$$ \binom{n}{k} = \frac{n!}{k!(n-k)!} \quad (\text{binomial coefficient}) $$

3.4 Calculus Formulas

$$ \frac{dy}{dx} = \lim_{\Delta x \to 0} \frac{f(x+\Delta x) - f(x)}{\Delta x} $$$$ \int_{a}^{b} f'(x) \, dx = f(b) - f(a) \quad (\text{Newton-Leibniz formula}) $$$$ \iint_{D} f(x,y) \, dx\,dy \quad (\text{double integral}) $$$$ \iiint_{\Omega} f(x,y,z) \, dx\,dy\,dz \quad (\text{triple integral}) $$$$ \oint_{C} P\,dx + Q\,dy = \iint_{D} \left( \frac{\partial Q}{\partial x} - \frac{\partial P}{\partial y} \right) dx\,dy \quad (\text{Green's theorem}) $$$$ \nabla \cdot \mathbf{F} = \frac{\partial F_x}{\partial x} + \frac{\partial F_y}{\partial y} + \frac{\partial F_z}{\partial z} \quad (\text{divergence}) $$$$ \nabla \times \mathbf{F} = \begin{vmatrix} \mathbf{i} & \mathbf{j} & \mathbf{k} \\ \frac{\partial}{\partial x} & \frac{\partial}{\partial y} & \frac{\partial}{\partial z} \\ F_x & F_y & F_z \end{vmatrix} \quad (\text{curl}) $$

3.5 Linear Algebra Formulas

$$ \mathbf{A} = \begin{pmatrix} a_{11} & a_{12} & \dots & a_{1n} \\ a_{21} & a_{22} & \dots & a_{2n} \\ \vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots \\ a_{m1} & a_{m2} & \dots & a_{mn} \end{pmatrix} $$$$ \det(\mathbf{A}) = \begin{vmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 3 & 4 \end{vmatrix} = 1 \times 4 - 2 \times 3 = -2 $$$$ \mathbf{A} \mathbf{x} = \lambda \mathbf{x} \quad (\text{eigenvalue equation}) $$$$ \operatorname{rank}(\mathbf{A}) = \operatorname{rank}(\mathbf{A}^T) $$$$ \mathbf{A}^{-1} = \frac{1}{\det(\mathbf{A})} \operatorname{adj}(\mathbf{A}) \quad (\text{inverse matrix}) $$$$ \| \mathbf{x} \|_2 = \sqrt{\sum_{i=1}^n x_i^2} \quad (\text{L2 norm}) $$

3.6 Set Theory Formulas

$$ A \cup B = \{x \mid x \in A \lor x \in B\} $$$$ A \cap B = \{x \mid x \in A \land x \in B\} $$$$ A \setminus B = \{x \mid x \in A \land x \notin B\} $$$$ \emptyset \subseteq A \subseteq U \quad (U \text{ is the universal set}) $$$$ \bigcup_{i=1}^{n} A_i,\quad \bigcap_{i=1}^{n} A_i $$$$ |A \cup B| = |A| + |B| - |A \cap B| \quad (\text{inclusion-exclusion principle}) $$$$ \mathcal{P}(A) = \{B \mid B \subseteq A\} \quad (\text{power set}) $$

3.9 Probability and Statistics
